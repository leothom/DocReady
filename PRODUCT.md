# SlideReady

register: product

SlideReady helps Sendelta teachers review spelling, grammar and the clarity of teaching content in PowerPoint slides. Both Chinese and native English teaching staff use it. The interface supports English and Simplified Chinese.

Teachers need a short, clear flow with little text: approved school email or private invite, choose/drop a PPTX, review, then one feedback slide at a time. Suggested text is copied manually into PowerPoint; the app does not edit the file. Subject and student TOEFL level are optional. Keep useful feedback prominent and operational details out of the teacher flow.

The design uses the existing pale background, dark green text and controls, familiar browser elements, clear labels and restrained motion. Preserve this design. Keep keyboard focus visible, labels explicit, errors readable and the layout usable on phones. Do not add a new design library or ornamental panels.

Approved-email access is immediate and does not verify mailbox ownership. The approved address list and usage reports are private owner data. Per-profile and global limits protect the AI account. The owner manages approval, expiry, limits, revocation and usage from /admin.

Original PPTX files are read locally. Editable slide text passes through the Hong Kong server to DeepSeek. SlideReady does not save source text or suggestions. It keeps approved email addresses and bounded usage records. Provider data policy is separate.
